## 2025-05-15 - [Cart & Product Accessibility Enhancements]
**Learning:** Icon-only buttons (like quantity adjusters and help toggles) are common in luxury interfaces but often lack accessible labels. Adding a clear CTA in empty states (like the cart) prevents dead-ends and improves conversion flow.
**Action:** Always ensure icon-only buttons have 'aria-label' and empty states provide a way back to the main shopping experience.

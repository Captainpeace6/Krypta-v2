## 2025-05-15 - Accessibility patterns for interactive elements
**Learning:** For a luxury-focused site with custom UI and icon-only buttons, accessibility (ARIA labels and `aria-current`) is crucial since visual indicators like focus rings might be suppressed or subtle.
**Action:** Always ensure icon-only buttons have descriptive `aria-label` attributes and that active navigation links are marked with `aria-current="page"` to aid screen reader users and maintain semantic clarity.

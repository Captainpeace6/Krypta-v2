Questions we get

What makes these "gothic jeans"?

The graphics and hardware — gothic embroidery, skull and baroque prints, studded hems, acid and sand washes — on a dark, oversized base. They read as gothic streetwear from across the room.

Are KRYPTAA jeans heavyweight?

Yes. The base denim is heavyweight and the pieces are washed once so they're broken in on day one without being thin.

How do I choose between men's and women's wide-leg?

Men's sit at the natural waist with a baggy leg; women's sit high on the waist with a straight, long leg. Several pieces are unisex — check the product page.

Need
to know:

01How does it fit?
Relaxed through the leg with intentional stacking. Size true for a clean fall or up for more volume. Tees: Oversized and boxy. Choose true size for a structured drape or size up for a more dramatic shoulder.
02How long does shipping take?
Orders process in 3–7 business days, then ship. US delivery is typically 7–14 days and international 10–20 days.
03Is shipping free?
Free standard shipping applies automatically on orders over $60, all regions.
04What's your return policy?
We accept returns within 7 days of delivery on unworn items with tags attached. Shipping & Returns →
05How do I exchange for another size?
We process exchanges as a refund plus a new order — return the original for a refund, then order the size you want.
